Privacy Policy — Daxon Mobility Driver Portal

Last updated: August 2026

Daxon Mobility ("we", "us", "our") operates the Driver Portal, which lets drivers check their salary status, advances, and upload their own documents. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • The mobile number you enter to look up your driver record — this is matched against records already held by Daxon Mobility as your employer/contracting business
  • Your driver record, which may include your name, phone number, address, photo, assigned vehicle, salary, attendance, and advance history
  • If you use "Edit My Info", any changes you submit — including your bank account details (bank name, account holder name, account number, IFSC code, branch) — are saved to your record for salary payment purposes
  • If you upload documents (Driving License, Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Agreement, Police Verification), those files are stored as part of your driver record

How this information is used

Information is used only to show you your salary and advance status, to generate your salary slips, and to keep your driver record (including bank and identity documents) up to date for payroll and compliance purposes. We do not use this information for advertising, and we do not sell or share it with third parties.

Sensitive information

Bank account details and identity documents (such as Aadhaar and PAN) are sensitive. We collect them only because they are necessary to pay your salary and meet standard employment record-keeping requirements. This information is stored using the same protected system as the rest of your driver record and is only accessible to the app and authorized Daxon Mobility staff.

Where data is stored

Data, including uploaded documents, is stored using Google Firebase (Realtime Database), a cloud infrastructure service, and is protected using authentication and access rules so that it cannot be read or changed except through this app.

Your rights

You can update your own details, including bank information, directly in the app, or contact us using the details below to ask about, correct, or request removal of your information.
